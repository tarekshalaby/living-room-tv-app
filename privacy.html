Privacy

Living Room TV runs on a single television in one home. When the household signs in with YouTube, the app reads the list of channels they subscribe to and those channels' latest videos (read-only access, scope youtube.readonly), only to show them on that TV.

Nothing is sold, shared, or sent to anyone else. Sign-in tokens stay on the TV. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
